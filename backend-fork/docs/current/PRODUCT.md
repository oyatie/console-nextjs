# Console product authority

Status: active product authority.

## Product boundary

Console builds one governed company object system in this order:

1. Ontology / Foundry-style object engine and deterministic policy.
2. Company, OrgUnit, JobPosition, Person, and Employment. People/recruiting directory create mints Person in the same transaction as the employees row (#966; `person_id = employee_id`). `hr.appoint` remains a distinct four-eyes assignment.
3. HR appointment, promotion, and transfer through one canonical assignment writer.
4. Payroll projected from existing payroll truth as PayRun. Draft calculate is admitted (`POST /api/v1/payroll/runs/{id}/calculate` already persists versioned won drafts; `payable` stays false). Korea compliance conclusions, wage-statement legal sign-off, and payment execution remain **HOLD**.
5. A Leptos SSR frontend. ADR-0030 substrate gates are green and `Layer::Ui` is accepted (ADR-0041); first full-depth vertical is payroll execution. GET `/_ui` serves deny-by-omission SSR (`console-payroll-ui` nested by `console-app`; #952). Unauthenticated and MEMBER get the empty shell; `PayrollRunRead` gets OpenAPI `PayrollRunSummary` required fields as `data-run-*` attributes, no won (#959). Those authorized runs are a Leptos `#[island]` (`AuthorizedRuns`, #962) hydrated by committed release WASM at `/_ui/pkg` (#964). Shipping screens (organization, HR, payroll) are composed on `/_ui` (#976); payroll drill-through hrefs and empty-vs-denied SSR landed (#982). Persona real-backend E2E on `/_ui`, `/_ui/organization`, `/_ui/hr`, and `/_ui/payroll` landed (#978). That is the ADR-0025 §4 bar for calling those GET screens shipped. Live exposure remains **HOLD**.

**Tenant is always a Group.** Every Company has a real `groups` row (Palantir-uniform account shape). A standalone 법인 is a Group of one, minted 1:1 with that Company. A conglomerate is the same Group object with multiple member Companies. A Company belongs to at most one Group (`UNIQUE (org_id)` on `group_memberships`). Group is not a legal person and is not the employer.

**Company is the legal entity and the RLS cell.** Employment, OrgUnit, JobPosition, PayRun, and Company business receipts always carry one `org_id`. Narrow Account identity/context and Group topology/capacity/operation control records have their own scoped owners and receipt namespaces; they never become mixed-Company payroll truth. Group never arms `app.current_org`. Writes stay one Company. Consolidated reads remain N× `with_org_conn`; empty grant → empty view.

**Cross-entity work is policy, not tenancy.** Staff of Company A and Company B may share an office; that does not merge OrgUnits or employments. If policy allows, an actor legally employed at B may operate member-company actions (including another company's PayRun) as a principal authorized on that Company — still that Company's `org_id`, four-eyes, and ledger. It is not one mixed group payroll.

The overlay already exists (migration 0060 `groups` / `group_memberships`, grants `GROUP_ADMIN` / `GROUP_VIEWER` / `GROUP_FINANCE`, fail-closed `group_member_org_ids`, `console-platform-group::consolidated_read`). Every Company has a real Group row and `organizations.group_id` is NOT NULL (#971); remove from a multi-member Group remints a Group-of-one. Group still does not arm `app.current_org`. OrgUnit kinds Site, Department, and Team under one Company are enforced in revision attributes (#975); parent cycles longer than 1 are refused (#981). Operational `sites` / `regions` / `branches` stay the auth spine and are not OrgUnits. It does not add `ObjectKey::Group` in this record. The canonical `ObjectKey` roster stays six until a later reviewed design proves Group identity writes need a port.

Console targets the functional breadth of Foundry, Ontology, Actions and Fusion as one native governed object platform, including data, lineage, collaborative work and operational continuity. This is a product target, not a completed equivalence claim. The existing Postgres + object-type substrate is the first integration and comparison target; storage versioning, concurrency control, ordering and replication remain evidence-based design decisions. A versioned capability-by-capability inventory with acceptance evidence is required before claiming full-suite equivalence. Console does not use Palantir SaaS or call Palantir APIs.

**Palantir AIP is the target** for the intelligence layer. That layer is built in the separate Intelligence repository until Intelligence has a named, SHA-bound stable base; cloning it into Console is then authorized. Until that clone lane, AI judgment remains out of Console's tenant product. Console-owned **seams** (fail-closed application port, same `Principal` and ontology actions, no second writer, no Palantir client) are admitted so a later clone can connect without rewriting Console. Owner lock (2026-08-29): those seams include **bind-only** Intelligence HTTP, fail-closed to `127.0.0.1` only, implemented as `GET http://127.0.0.1:<listen-port>/internal/intelligence/bind` on the existing listener (#972). That route is not in published OpenAPI, not under `/_ui`, and not under `/api/v1`. Unset or non-loopback bind is fail-closed. No chat, no inference text in tenant UX, no HR/payroll write, no autonomous merge. Intelligence is not a tenant application and is not autonomous merge authority. Do not call Palantir APIs.

The first implementation vertical is native nonpayable payroll with its required Account/current-context, policy, draft/action, evidence-custody, workflow/transfer, review, historical own-publication/correction and selected-Company Group-operation dependencies, as bound to the separately approved integrated design SHA. Applicant, former worker, partner and internal access use current resource/field/action policy without hardcoded job titles; persistent Account identity is independent of Employment. Bounded internal evidence custody is admitted as a dependency, not as a separately shipped ingest/evidence product. Complete HR/attendance/labor/payroll stories, native messenger/mail/calendar, approved external sharing and enterprise collaborative sheets remain explicit later product commitments, requiring their own coherent designs and test/implementation admission. ERP/finance and unrelated vertical implementation remains outside this first slice. Payment execution, legal/compliance conclusions and production exposure retain their separate HOLDs. Existing code in later modules is not automatic shipping authority.

## Product invariants

- Commands are deterministic and revision-aware, with replay-safe receipts and auditable mutations.
- Tenant isolation, deny-by-omission, and nondisclosure apply at every read and write boundary. The tenant **account** is always a Group (standalone Company = Group of one with a real row). The RLS **cell** is always a Company (`org_id` / `app.current_org`). Group never arms that GUC.
- Effective-dated truth uses half-open intervals; history is closed and appended, never overwritten.
- Projected objects have exactly one domain writer. Ontology and adapters do not create alternate write paths.
- Requester and approver are distinct natural persons for `company.*`, `hr.*`, and `payroll.*` approvals, even when their capacities differ. All other kinds — including `organization.*` and `people.*` — hold only the account-level `approver_id <> requested_by` bar. `requires_natural_person_four_eyes` (`backend/crates/governance/domain`) is prefix-scoped and a test asserts the exclusion. Extending the bar to the remaining kinds is **unscheduled, not blocked**: migration 0076 shows only that `users.employee_id` is nullable, and a NULL resolution can fail closed exactly as the enforced kinds already do. Whether unbound accounts actually exist is an open question no census has answered, so the compatibility risk is a hypothesis on HOLD rather than a reason to leave six of thirteen dispatch targets outside the invariant.
- Preflight uses the same authorization, policy, state, revision, and input validation as execute and performs no mutation.
- Legal sources are versioned evidence, not transferable compliance conclusions. Production exposure and compliance claims require separate authority.
- The repository integration and source-release branch is `dev`. Ordered environment mirrors are `dev` → `staging` → `canary` → `production`. Creating or fast-forwarding those names is not production exposure.

## Architecture

The existing Rust backend is reused as verified substrate rather than rewritten wholesale. The product exposes governed company objects and authorized actions:

`Company → OrgUnit(Site/Department/Team/…) → JobPosition → Person/Employment → HR action → PayRun`

The tenant account is always a Group (standalone = Group of one with a real row). Group is not a node on this legal chain and not an `ObjectKey`. OrgUnit kinds live under one Company; a shared physical office is not a shared OrgUnit. REST and any future server functions are *intended as* sibling adapters over the same application-layer use cases. This is an aspiration, not an enforced boundary: `Layer::allowed_deps` lets `Layer::Rest` reach Adapter, Platform, Domain and Kernel directly, and 6 of the 34 non-platform REST crates (analytics-quant, consulting, facilities, logistics, orgchange, production) declare no application dependency at all. Counting every package whose name ends `-rest` the ratio is 9 of 37: `console-platform-auth-rest`, `console-platform-authz-rest` and `console-platform-rest` also declare none, and are excluded above only because the layer gate classifies `crates/platform/*` as Platform before it reaches the `-rest` suffix. Converging them is unscheduled work. The frontend reads real contracts, omits unauthorized data server-side, and contains no client-side business authority. Cargo is the target build system; existing Buck paths remain repository reality until a dedicated, evidence-backed convergence change removes them without losing test coverage. `-ui` members are skipped by first-party Buck generation until Leptos is vendored.

## Integrated first-vertical design admission proposal

This proposed text becomes active only through reviewed current-authority work bound to the approved design SHA; the evidence candidate alone is not authority. Registered common review decisions inherit the underlying domain natural-person independence rule, regardless of transport prefix. Payroll submitter/decider independence and optional gate requester/approver independence remain distinct. Every selected new projected owner is explicitly enumerated in the approved action/type manifest; the historical six-key roster is the current deployed baseline, not permission to silently grow a runtime enum. Account, scoped CompanyActor attribution and required native source/control types are admitted only through their exact owner/codec/migration/projection enrollment, with legacy aliases delegating or refusing.

Current implementation facts above remain facts about the deployed repository; proposed future behavior is not described as already shipped. First-vertical design/test approval does not assert support for every employment/pay circumstance among the2,000-person pilot or for50,000/1,000,000-person operation. Unsupported required cases remain visible, incomplete and assigned to accountable repair; they cannot be removed from the selected population to create apparent completion.

Selected proposed canonical projected additions for this design are ContractWage, WorkCalendar, CalendarAssignment, AttendanceCoverage, AttendanceCloseBasis, SourceQualification, EligibilityAssessment and TaxEvidence, each with its existing payroll/attendance owner and exact source-schema projection. Common drafts and governed definition/content types remain instance-backed; Account/Group authority has separate scoped owners. This enumeration supersedes the six-only restriction for this approved first-vertical design once this authority proposal is adopted; it does not say these additions are implemented today.
