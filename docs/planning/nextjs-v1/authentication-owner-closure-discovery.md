Legacy classification must account for every Account and open/terminal source. Current keys plus0229positive removal evidence and preserved historical evidence inform conservative classification. All pre-existing zero-key Accounts are unresolved/recovery-required; never-enrolled is reserved for genuinely new allocated UUIDs. Apply the accepted design's explicit legacy table: an active Account with a supported valid key retains its current valid family IDs and deadlines at a recorded legacy generation; a zero-key or inactive Account does not gain that continuation. Record legacy purpose/provenance without inventing a source ceremony, human proof, or fresh admission. New issuance cannot infer authority from key presence. Preserve original IDs/key/RP bytes, source and terminal evidence, strict expiries and prior ledger metadata; ambiguous rows require repair.

Do not move title-only credential reset or shared ForceReset self-handoff into unrestricted new command authority. The accepted design requires the compatible reset alias to produce actual protected recovery work, followed by attended evidence, exact independent decision and staged-key execution. Cold-start resurrection is removed only as the real empty-install Group-of-one signup/passkey/assent/free-entitlement flow and separate witnessed commissioning replace its product role. No disabled feature shell or fabricated verified human is an acceptable replacement. Intermediate source work remains unexposed and incomplete until this transition's complete producer/consumer and SSR path passes.
