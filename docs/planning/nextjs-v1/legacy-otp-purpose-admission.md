## Observed behavior and owner reuse

The two existing authentication regressions demonstrate that an OTP enrollment bearer can create ordinary work, including after registration and refresh. All live passkey, OTP and device-login writers still mint provenance-version-0 families. Migration0240 and OTP redemption already atomically bind each newly redeemed OTP family to its exact source, Account and home Company. Refresh preserves that family ID. Shared request-context validation currently ignores the binding.

Reuse this positive binding in the existing shared request owner. Do not infer normal authentication from key count, setup flags, role/title, or absence of a binding. Unbound version-0 families retain inherited compatibility; their historical proof remains unclassified. Version-1 families stay unavailable until the separate generation/admission/old-writer cutover. No migration, signing codec, family identity, dependency, public DTO or new authentication engine is planned.

## Required behavior

1. `validate_current_claims` remains the default ordinary-admission entry. Under the original family's home Company, its existing authoritative read also checks provenance and the positive OTP-family binding. Any bound OTP family is denied for business access, even when its source is consumed, expired, revoked or missing. Binding lookup must not disappear because its source or Account fields differ. A same-Company malformed binding fails closed. Current Account, roles, generation, Company, family, expiry, delegation and target-policy checks remain.
2. Provenance-version-1 families are rejected by both admission paths. This repair cannot release dormant admissions. Legacy version-0 families with no marker preserve existing validation behavior; that is compatibility, not a claim of proven normal purpose.
3. Provide a narrow explicitly named enrollment-capable entry that shares the current validator. It accepts only direct own-Account sessions, never delegated/view-as/read-only sessions. Auth REST selects it only for registration start/finish, own passkey summaries, privacy status/acceptance and passkey enrollment handoff. All other credential, admin, mobile step-up and device-login session-approval handlers use ordinary admission. Preserve direct platform initial enrollment and consent, including the existing platform-home exception; platform administration is ordinary admission.
4. Registration and enrollment handoff retain exact original-source/ceremony checks and existing genuine passkey step-up requirements for adding a device. Preserve the zero-key desktop-to-phone enrollment handoff and source-replacement flows. No bearer is promoted on registration. Passkey summaries expose only the subject's existing own summary fields and are not a business-directory exception.
5. Session-based device-login approval must require ordinary admission at the HTTP boundary and recheck the same restriction under its existing Account/family locks before mutating the handoff. OTP plus a newly present key must not become a new desktop family. Fresh signed WebAuthn approval and fresh passkey login remain legitimate paths. Correct the inherited flow description that suggests an OTP session alone can approve after enrollment.
6. Ordinary, Group, platform, already-minted delegated actor and realtime admission inspect the original family and home Company through this shared owner. Do not add guards at individual business endpoints or replace target Cedar checks with source purpose.
7. Refresh retains the same family. `requires_passkey_setup` keeps its current key-absence DTO meaning and never grants ordinary admission. Source-deadline clamping, protected version-1 generation ownership, attended recovery and transactional revocation across every domain write remain separate HOLDs; do not claim these are repaired.
8. Previously session-approved device handoffs do not record their approving family or purpose. This patch prevents new OTP-based session approvals; it cannot classify a pre-change approval that later polls into an unbound version-0 family. Activation/migration stays on HOLD until those old handoffs have safely expired/drained or separately reviewed reconciliation establishes their authority. No live cutover is performed or claimed here.
