# Approval independence: remove title exemptions

Base: accepted candidate `eebe6c0143ffb2e62a949911c4bc883ac64784ac1fa2f9b8463547683b75da4f`. Root is the only source writer. This is a necessary correction within full R4, not completion of natural-person verification, the semantic high-risk registry or B09.

R4 requires a requester plus a different authorized natural-person approver, including Company owner/admin/one-person cases. Four existing owner paths explicitly waive their same-account guard for `is_org_lead` or `SUPER_ADMIN`: financial purchase approval, workflow waiting-task decisions, staged workflow revision publication, and shared document/benefit lifecycle approval. Those paths write an anomaly after allowing the prohibited effect. A finding cannot substitute for prevention. The current governance natural-person resolver follows a personnel-directory binding only; it is not attended identity proofing and must not be advertised as verified human identity.

## Required behavior and ownership

Deny self-approval for every title at these existing approval boundaries. Do not add a second approval or require a routine third person. A different authorized account remains the inherited distinct-actor path pending the separately required verified-person owner; passing it is not proof of distinct humans.
2. Financial: retain both requested_by and submitted_by as maker facts. Admin and executive approval require the actor to differ from both. Remove title lookup/exemption/finding creation from the self-approval guard. Preserve the existing validation error category/Korean message for same-actor denial. Before target normalization, pin the private owner action to its locked from-state: expenditure preparation only from AdminApproved, executive approval only from ExecutivePending. Genuine approval pairs are RequestSubmitted→AdminApproved and ExecutivePending→ReadyToExecute. Routing from AdminApproved to ReadyToExecute/ExecutivePending is permitted to the requester/submitter only after a non-NULL recorded admin approver distinct from both requested_by and non-NULL submitted_by. Historical exempt or missing approval records remain preserved but cannot confer new routing authority. Existing requested_by is NOT NULL; missing submitted_by fails closed for genuine approval and routing. Final execution remains a separately authorized path; its complete current natural-person/receipt gate remains required in R4.
3. Workflow waiting decisions: only APPROVE requires an identified run initiator different from the deciding actor. Missing initiated_by fails closed. Rejection/return, author finalization, receipt confirmation and task claiming retain their dedicated paths. Preserve retry of an already accepted decision as historical receipt lookup, without creating another effect; do not rewrite old exempt decisions or findings.
4. Workflow pending revision: require a recorded pending_staged_by different from the approver. A NULL staging actor cannot become a bypass. Keep the pending revision, active version and events unchanged on refusal. Existing definition-manage and real passkey step-up remain mandatory. Withdrawal remains an author operation. This correction does not certify initial publication, personal-vs-Company semantic classification, payload binding or the later verified human gate.
5. Shared lifecycle: for the existing CHECKER_TRANSITIONS only, require a recorded non-NULL maker and identified non-NULL approver, then reject equality regardless of title. Preserve all non-checker author/publish/retirement transitions, hold/retention guards, and all historical transition/finding evidence. Delete the exception branch, not the findings table or unrelated detectors. Missing attribution is a denial, not a guessed identity.
6. Reuse the responsible owner guard. Do not introduce a parallel approval service or title policy. Preserve public response categories for existing denials. New absent-maker cases use forbidden. No migration, source/identity rekeying, token or external effect change is required.

## Risk and rollback

Pre-mortem: delete one exemption while another remains; allow unknown makers; confuse routing with approval; update assertions to hide an authorization/fixture failure; deny legitimate author finalization; rewrite historical receipts. Detection: actual owner/runtime-role operations, negative state/evidence counts, and positive continuation/retry controls. Blast radius is the four owner boundaries and focused tests. Source-only rollback cannot reopen the old unauthorized path; a regression is fixed forward or the affected live executor remains unexposed. No deployment is authorized.

Stop on weakened isolation, lost history, changed natural-person identities, accidental blanket approvals for low-risk actions, deadlock or a changed public success contract. Independent design and test review precede implementation. Lenses: Red Team, Operability/Day-2, Blast radius, Zero trust, Essentialism. Full R4 remains the end state: verified Account/Person linkage, non-weakenable semantic action classification, exact approval digest/current authority and two-site receipts are still mandatory work after this correction.
