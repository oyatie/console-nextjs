## Exact boundary

Add migration 0228 without modifying migrations 0–227. One migration-owned private `auth_security.account_id_reservations` table records only `account_id UUID PRIMARY KEY` and `retired BOOLEAN NOT NULL`. Backfill every existing Account UUID with `retired=false`; this records allocated identity, not enrollment, verified humanity or authority. No timestamp, Company, profile, key, secret or matching attribute belongs in this minimal table. It is the permanent UUID denial set: once allocated, that UUID cannot be allocated again, including after retirement. Detailed security state and its separate retention schedule follow in the next dependency.

The existing minimum retired-UUID retention limitation also applies to this allocation set. No serving role can read, insert, update, delete, truncate or grant access to it. No serving role owns its schema/table/functions or can disable its triggers. It remains outside public Company-removal scans and has no FK to deletable roots. The migration owner alone backfills, and owner-only triggers/retirement helpers apply ongoing changes. No new runtime role or command privilege is introduced.

The migration holds `users` SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE before backfill and through trigger installation/commit, so no concurrent writer can escape reservation. `users` AFTER INSERT reserves NEW.id with an unconditional INSERT into that private primary key (no reset/upsert). Native uniqueness supplies absent-row concurrency protection even when a caller retains an old REPEATABLE READ snapshot. A lookup of retired rows alone is insufficient. The AFTER trigger runs only for inserted Accounts: `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING/UPDATE` creates no phantom allocation. Rolled-back allocation reserves nothing. Account UUID and home Company UPDATE are rejected by a pure OLD/NEW trigger; ordinary profile, role and active-state changes remain with existing owners.

`users` BEFORE DELETE marks its existing reservation retired before cascades. A missing reservation fails closed; repeated retirement of the same existing reservation is idempotent. The mark and deletion share the caller transaction. Both ordinary and force Company-removal functions call a private sorted retirement helper after their final eligibility gate and before their first child sweep. Never retire in the 0227 lock helper: blocked removal must preserve live identity. Existing Account-first locks, NOWAIT upgrades, outcome strings, audit ownership and final ACLs remain unchanged. A failed sweep or audit rolls back retirement along with deletion. This prerequisite does not authorize former-worker destruction; that wider R4 correction remains required.
